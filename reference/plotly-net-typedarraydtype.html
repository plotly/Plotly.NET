TypedArrayDType Type

Namespace: Plotly.NET
Assembly: Plotly.NET.dll
Base Type: obj
All Interfaces: IEquatable<TypedArrayDType> , IStructuralEquatable , IComparable<TypedArrayDType> , IComparable , IStructuralComparable

Data type tag for encoded typed arrays accepted by plotly.js (>= 2.28.0). Serializes to the shorthand strings documented in the plotly.js release notes (e.g. "f8" for Float64, "u1c" for UInt8Clamped).
